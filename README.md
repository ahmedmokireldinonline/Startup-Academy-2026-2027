## Quick wins

These are **not acceptance guarantees**. They are the lowest-friction starting points based on the published rules in this snapshot.

- **NVIDIA Inception** — free, no equity, no deadline or cohort, and no revenue requirement; incorporation and a developer are required.
- **SendPulse Startup Grant** — free-plan startups can apply; the official page states a 1–3 business-day review.
- **Google for Startups Cloud Program** — a pre-funded path is published with up to $2,000 in credits.
- **AWS Activate Founders** — self-funded startups can apply directly, starting with $1,000 in credits.

## Status legend

- ✅ **Open** — a current application route is explicitly presented.
- 🔄 **Rolling** — ongoing applications or no cohort deadline is published.
- ⏳ **Opens soon** — the official source says a call is coming but does not yet provide an application deadline.
- ❌ **Closed** — not used in the active table; closed entries belong in the archive.

## Maintenance

Every active row has a primary official source and a last-verified date. Unknown details are labeled **Not published** rather than inferred. Review entries at least every 90 days and move entries to [archive](archive/unverified.md) when the official source no longer supports an active application path.
