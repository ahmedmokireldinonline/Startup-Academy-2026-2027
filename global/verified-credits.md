<a id="india-credit-guarantee"></a>

<details>
<summary><strong>🔄 Rolling Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups</strong> — Government of India</summary>

**Apply / source:** [https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/credit-guarantee-scheme-for-startups.html](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/credit-guarantee-scheme-for-startups.html)  
**Last verified:** 2026-10-14

- **Benefit:** Guarantee cover up to ₹20 crore per eligible borrower. Transaction-based cover is 85% of default for loan amounts up to ₹10 crore and 75% above ₹10 crore, subject to the scheme rules.
- **Eligibility:** DPIIT-recognised startup, not in default or classified as NPA, with eligibility certified by a Member Institution.
- **Route:** This is not a direct cash credit from the government. The startup approaches an eligible bank, NBFC, or venture debt fund, which applies for the guarantee.
- **Deadline:** Rolling lender/Jan Samarth route; no annual deadline published.
- **Code:** No official public code found.

**How to apply:** Confirm DPIIT recognition → check eligibility on Jan Samarth or with a Member Institution → prepare business and borrowing documents → apply for debt financing → lender handles the guarantee-cover process.

</details>

<details>

## Important distinction

Cloud credits reduce an infrastructure bill. Grants and subsidies reimburse eligible costs or fund development. Tax offsets are claimed through tax filings and may require eligible expenditure before the benefit is received. Credit guarantees support a lender’s debt decision and are not the same as a cash grant.
