## Details and application roadmaps

### Vercel for Startups

- **Benefit:** Up to $30,000 flexible commitment credits for one year. The commitment may cover seats, data transfer, compute, AI Gateway, v0, and other applicable Vercel services. Up to 50% of the monthly allocation may be used for AI Gateway.
- **Eligibility:** Affiliation with an approved Vercel Startup Partner; Series A or earlier; application within 12 months of the latest funding round; public company website; matching work email; proof of partner affiliation; no previous Vercel Startup credits.
- **How to apply:** Select or create a Vercel team → choose the approved partner → upload proof → submit → accept the startup terms if approved.
- **Timeline:** Review is typically 5–7 days; benefits activate after terms are accepted.
- **Code:** No public code. This is an approval-based partner program.

### Miro Startup Program

- **Benefit:** $1,000 credit for startups connected to a Miro global partner; $500 may be available through the independent application route.
- **Eligibility:** Free Miro account; work email; partner route or independent eligibility review. The official application page confirms both credit levels.
- **How to apply:** Create a free account → use the partner link or official application → provide eligibility information → remain on the free plan during review → apply the credit after approval.
- **Timeline:** The detailed official requirements state that independent requests may take up to 15 days.
- **Code:** No public code. Partner access or the official application is required.

### ElevenLabs Startup Grants

- **Benefit:** 33 million characters, more than 680 hours of Agents usage, 12 months of access, and community/founder support. The provider states a value of more than $5,500.
- **Eligibility:** Monetized long-term product or business model; startup or company with fewer than 25 employees; valid business email; no one-off projects, minors' projects, agencies, consulting firms, or SMBs; one application per company.
- **How to apply:** Complete the official grant application → describe the team, product, and growth plan → await review → receive grant-plan credits if accepted.
- **Timeline:** Rolling applications; provider says it normally responds within one week.
- **Code:** No public code.

### Airtable for Startups

- **Benefit:** $500 free Airtable credit plus exclusive startup content and educational materials.
- **Eligibility and documents:** Create an Airtable account first; provide the email associated with it, name, LinkedIn profile, Airtable workspace ID, company name, and company LinkedIn if available.
- **How to apply:** Create an account → identify the workspace ID → complete the official form → wait for review → credit is added to the workspace if approved.
- **Timeline:** The official form says review may take 3–5 days.
- **Code:** No public code.

### Deepgram for Startups

- **Benefit:** Up to $100,000 in Deepgram credits for 12 months, plus startup community, networking, and engineering support.
- **Eligibility:** AI builder or early-stage startup that is already in production or plans to launch within six months and will actively build and engage with the community.
- **How to apply:** Create a Deepgram account → keep the Project ID → submit the startup application → await review → credits are deposited into the console after acceptance.
- **Timeline:** Typical response within one week; credits usually appear within 24–48 hours after acceptance.
- **Code:** No public code.

### Vultr Promotional Credits

- **Official codes and benefits:**
  - `VULTRMATCH`: matches the first deposit dollar-for-dollar up to $100.
  - `FLYTWOHUNDRED`: $200 free credit.
  - `FLY300VULTR`: $300 free credit.
  - `250VULTRFLY`: $250 free credit.
- **Eligibility:** New customers only. Promotional credit applies to selected products, cannot be combined with other offers, and is subject to Vultr's terms.
- **How to apply:** Open the official coupon page → select one offer → create a new account → enter the code where instructed → review the offer terms before provisioning resources.
- **Deadline:** The official coupon page does not publish a universal expiry date; verify at checkout.
- **Important:** These are general cloud promotions, not equity-free startup grants or startup-program approval.
